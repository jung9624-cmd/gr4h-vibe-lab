What changed?
Increasing x4 spreads effective rainfall over a longer unit hydrograph, typically delaying and attenuating the hydrograph peak. Halving x4 raises the peak to 6.33 m³/s (lag 2h); doubling it lowers the peak to 2.61 m³/s (lag 10h).
